About Msizi's Mobile Wash

Built on a love for cars and a simple idea: your time matters more than a trip to the car wash.

Our Story

Msizi Gumede grew up in KwaZulu-Natal, where a fascination with cars turned into a habit of detailing his own vehicle every weekend. After relocating to Johannesburg, Msizi noticed how little time the city's professionals had to look after their cars properly. Msizi's Mobile Wash was started to close that gap — bringing the same care Msizi gave his own car directly to homes, offices, and residential complexes across the city.

Mission

To deliver premium, convenient vehicle cleaning services directly to clients' homes, offices, and residential complexes across Johannesburg.

Vision

To become Johannesburg's most trusted on-demand mobile car care brand, known for reliability, quality, and customer-first service.
